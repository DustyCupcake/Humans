Where do the important links go on a phone?

Mock-up of the page actions on phones section of the navigation spec. Each phone is the real responsive page at 344 px wide. The proposed column also shows the bottom tab bar from the navigation proposal; “today” keeps the current scrolling link strip.

Team page

TodayActions and contact sit in a side column that stacks after the main content
  • The contact point and next meeting come after the whole task list.
  • Nothing is reachable without scrolling a long way.
ProposedHeader actions, at-a-glance cards first, sticky action bar
  • Title and primary action are at the top, as on desktop.
  • Contact and next meeting are a swipeable strip above the tasks, with a "Swipe" hint, a peeking next card and dots.
  • The decisive action is pinned above the bottom tab bar, which tucks away while you read and returns on scroll up.
  • Jump chips and collapsed cards shorten the page.

Task page

TodayWhat the task grants and whether it fits sit in the side column, after everything else
  • You can reach “Ask to pick up” before ever seeing what access it gives you.
ProposedThe decision information comes first; the action stays pinned
  • Access and fit are a swipeable strip right under the title, with the same cues.
  • “Ask to pick up” and “Shadow” stay pinned above the tab bar.
  • Following and staffing controls stay in the context area, last.

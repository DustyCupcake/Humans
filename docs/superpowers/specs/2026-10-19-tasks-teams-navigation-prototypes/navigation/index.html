Open questions this mock-up makes visible
  • Group names and membership. Are “Get involved / The event / Association” the right words for this community? Rideshare and Calendar could sit in either of the first two.
  • Home. The logo is home; should there also be a labelled Home item?
  • Pins. Browser-only is simplest; account-level pins follow you across devices but add export and erasure work.
  • Click or hover to open a group menu (click is shown; it works on touch and for keyboard users).
